THE CROSSPOSTING WORKSPACE

One source post.
Multiple destinations.

FREE CROSSPOSTER is a social media crossposting workspace designed to help creators prepare content once, organize destination-specific drafts and manage publishing from one place.

Content creation studio

Prepare captions and upload photos or videos in one workspace. Preview media, organize creative settings and keep the original source content attached to your draft.

Destination-aware drafts

Choose connected social accounts for each post. Keep platform destinations and their publishing status organized instead of copying the same content manually between tabs.

Publishing calendar

Review upcoming scheduled destinations and keep planned content visible. Scheduling availability depends on the connected platform and supported publishing permissions.

Publishing history

Review publishing events and destination results. Separate platform outcomes help you understand where a post succeeded or needs attention.

Connected account management

Authorize supported social accounts and manage the connections used by your workspace. Platform availability depends on provider approval and account eligibility.

Privacy-first workspace

Your drafts and connected account information belong in your authenticated workspace. Public pages explain the service; private content is not intended for public search indexing.

Platform availability

Instagram and TikTok workflows are present in the current application, subject to platform permissions and account configuration. YouTube and Facebook creation settings are being prepared; do not assume those destinations are available for live publishing until their integrations are completed.
