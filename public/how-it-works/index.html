FROM CREATION TO DISTRIBUTION

How crossposting
works.

Crossposting means preparing a piece of content once and distributing it to more than one social destination. FREE CROSSPOSTER organizes that workflow around one source post and separate destination records.

01 · Create one source post

Start with a photo, video or caption. Keep the original content in your workspace so you can manage it as one post instead of maintaining disconnected copies.

02 · Prepare each platform

Review the content format and destination-specific details. Different social networks have different media, caption, privacy and publishing requirements, so one universal upload cannot safely assume every platform behaves the same way.

03 · Select connected destinations

Choose the social accounts that are authorized and available in your workspace. A destination that is not connected or approved should remain clearly marked as unavailable.

04 · Publish and track results

Save drafts, publish where supported and review each destination's result in publishing history. A successful post on one platform does not automatically mean every destination succeeded.
