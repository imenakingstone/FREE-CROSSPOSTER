How to request deletion of your FREE CROSSPOSTER data.

You can request deletion of your FREE CROSSPOSTER account and information associated with it, including saved drafts, connected social account records, inbox conversations and messages, publishing history and profile details.

How to request deletion

  1. Email imenaking@gmail.com from the email address associated with your FREE CROSSPOSTER account.
  2. Use the subject line “FREE CROSSPOSTER Data Deletion”.
  3. Tell us whether you want to delete your entire account or only specific information.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What happens next

We will review and process verified requests within the period required by applicable law. Some records may be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution or legitimate operational reasons. Backups may persist for a limited period before being overwritten.

Deleting data from FREE CROSSPOSTER does not remove content already published to Instagram, Facebook or another connected platform. You must delete that content directly on the relevant platform.
