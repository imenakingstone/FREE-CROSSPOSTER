Understand the FREE CROSSPOSTER workflow, connected social accounts and platform availability.

What is FREE CROSSPOSTER?

FREE CROSSPOSTER is a web-based social media content workspace. It helps you prepare a source post, organize social destinations, manage drafts and review publishing activity.

What does crossposting mean?

Crossposting is distributing the same source content to multiple social platforms or accounts. Each destination may require its own format, settings, authorization and publishing result.

Which platforms can I connect?

The current application includes Instagram and TikTok workflows. Availability and public publishing depend on platform authorization, provider review and the permissions granted to your connected account. YouTube and Facebook integrations are not yet available for live publishing.

Can I publish to every platform at once?

The workspace is designed to organize multiple destinations, but each platform is processed separately. A destination must be connected and supported before publishing can take place.

Are my drafts public?

No. Drafts and connected account information are part of your authenticated workspace. Do not share your login credentials or authorization links.

Why might a platform reject a post?

Platforms enforce their own media specifications, account eligibility, publishing permissions and API limits. Check the destination-specific result in publishing history and correct the issue before retrying.

How do I delete my account data?

Follow the instructions on our data deletion page or contact support.
