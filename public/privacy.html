This Privacy Policy explains how FREE CROSSPOSTER ("we", "us" or "the Service") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our social media publishing workspace.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and information you provide for your profile.
  • Social account information: identifiers, usernames, display names, profile images and authorization details returned by a platform when you connect an account.
  • Authorization tokens: access and refresh tokens needed to perform actions you request. These are stored using server-side encryption.
  • Content and publishing data: photos, videos, captions, drafts, schedules, selected destinations, publishing results and error details.
  • Technical information: basic request, diagnostic and security information generated when the Service operates.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure your account; connect the social accounts you choose; store drafts and media; publish or schedule content at your direction; show publishing history and notifications; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse; and maintain and improve the Service.

3. Social platforms and sharing

When you connect a social platform, we send that platform the authorization requests and content needed to provide the features you choose. Content is sent to the destination account only when you request publishing or enable a supported scheduled action. Each platform processes information under its own privacy policy.

We use Supabase for application authentication, database and file storage infrastructure. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers acting to support the Service. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers for their advertising purposes.

4. Storage and security

We use access controls, database policies and server-side protection measures intended to limit access to user data. Social authorization tokens are encrypted server-side. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account, content and publishing information for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with applicable law. Media and records may remain until you delete them or request account deletion, subject to backup cycles and legal requirements. We are preparing a user-facing deletion process; until it is available, contact us to request deletion and we will review the request.

Disconnecting a social account revokes or removes the connection where supported, but may not automatically delete content already published to that platform. You can also revoke the Service's access in the platform's own settings.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose which social accounts to connect and what content to submit. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

7. International processing

Our infrastructure and connected platforms may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the Service or the connected platforms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date.
