Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FREE CROSSPOSTER ("we", "us" or "the Service") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our social media publishing workspace.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and profile details you provide.
  • Social account information: identifiers, usernames, display names and profile details returned when you connect a platform.
  • Authorization information: access and refresh tokens or related credentials needed to perform actions you request.
  • Content and publishing data: photos, videos, captions, drafts, schedules, selected destinations, publishing results and error details.
  • Messaging data (when you enable the Unified Inbox): message text, sender or recipient platform identifiers, message timestamps, delivery details and attachment metadata for conversations received through connected Instagram Professional accounts or Facebook Pages. We use this information only to show and manage conversations you choose to connect.
  • Technical information: basic diagnostic, request and security information generated while operating the Service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; connect social accounts you choose; store drafts and media; publish or schedule content at your direction where supported; show publishing history; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse; and maintain and improve the Service.

3. Social platforms and service providers

When you connect a social platform, we exchange authorization information and the content needed to provide the features you choose. Content is sent to a destination account when you request publishing or enable a supported scheduled action. If you enable the Unified Inbox, message events and message content delivered by Meta's approved messaging APIs may be stored in your workspace so you can read and reply. Replies are sent only when you request them and only when the platform's permissions and messaging window allow it. Each platform processes information under its own privacy policy.

We use Supabase for application authentication, database and file-storage infrastructure, and Vercel to host the web application. Information may be processed by these providers to operate the Service. We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and security

We use access controls and server-side protections intended to limit access to account information and social authorization data. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account, content and publishing information for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with applicable law. Backups may persist for a limited period after deletion. To request access, correction or deletion of your information, email imenaking@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Disconnecting a social account may remove its connection from the Service but does not delete content already published to that platform. Inbox messages stored before disconnection may remain until you request deletion. You can also revoke the Service's access in the platform's own settings. To request deletion of your FREE CROSSPOSTER account and associated inbox data, use the instructions at Data Deletion or email imenaking@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and rights

You choose which social accounts to connect and what content to submit. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

7. International processing

Our infrastructure providers and connected platforms may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the Service or connected platforms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: imenaking@gmail.com.
